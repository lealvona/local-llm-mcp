What this is

Private terms are literal strings that local-llm-mcp always replaces with a placeholder ([PERSON-1], [EMAIL-2]) before anything leaves this machine, in every mode. Shape detection already catches many e-mails, phones and addresses; terms catch what has no shape, like your name, your family's names, your handles, the street you live on.

How the suggestions below were made, and where your answers go

Suggestions come from this machine only: a seed file your deployment may provide, your git identity and your account's full name, plus name variants. They are guesses: correct the value if it is close, reject it if it is wrong. Accepted terms are written to the private terms file (mode 0600) on this machine. Rejected suggestions are remembered as one-way hashes, so they do not come back and are not stored in plain text. Nothing on this page is sent anywhere else.
